## Problem

CineV was good at maintaining consistent state in a 3D world and structuring scene context for an LLM. But video generation models made rapid gains in expressive range and physical consistency in 2026. Unreal-based rendering could no longer offset its narrower range of expression.

The work went well beyond swapping engines. We had to remove three years of legacy while carrying forward CineV's strengths in state and context management. We also had to rebuild a product that external users could not easily access directly as a web product centered on image and video generation models.

## Approach

We moved rendering to image and video generation models while making CineV's 3D state and context management capabilities part of the new product's foundation. Our hypothesis was that controllable conditioning from web-based 3D could carry more of the complex conditions that text alone struggles to express.

To turn that hypothesis into evidence the organization could use, the AI development lead and I compared text prompts with 3D conditioning across 120+ matching scenes. Five reviewers examined the results together and reached consensus judgments. The findings helped align the CPO, CTO, and engineering organization around the product transition.

## Execution

### Direction and Validation

In early February 2026, I began making the case for the product transition and building internal agreement. The CPO, CTO, and I jointly defined its scope and priorities. The AI development lead and I shared responsibility for setting the hypothesis, designing the experiment, interpreting the results, and communicating them across the organization. Development started on March 16, and Web CineV officially launched on July 10.

### Product Integration

We reworked the user journey from logline input through script, assets, storyboard, video generation, cut editing, and final Export. Along the way, we identified the work of assembling context bundles and inconsistencies between generated clips as major sources of friction. I prototyped Prompt Enhancer, Voice Adaptor, and Reverse Storyboard. After qualitative evaluation by internal creators, I handed the prototypes and validation findings to engineering, which integrated them into the product.

### Technology and Organization

I tracked new models and technologies through reliable official announcements, assessed whether they fit the existing product APIs or needed separate implementation, and passed those assessments to the web and AI teams. External model routing brought 30+ models into the product across 7+ areas. A separate Enablement project gave the organization a foundation for evaluating and validating new technologies directly.
