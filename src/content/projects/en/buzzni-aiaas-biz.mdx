## Problem
We needed a business structure that could bring scattered AI capabilities together under a single revenue model.

## Approach
We designed the product lineup, pricing, and operations organization in parallel to speed up GTM execution.

## Execution
- Redefined the AIaaS product portfolio
- Clarified responsibilities within the team structure (product planning/operations/development)
- Standardized sales-to-operations handoff documentation
