## Problem
We needed to bring scattered AI capabilities into a single revenue model and build a scalable AIaaS business for the organization's next stage of growth. Staff whose roles had become unclear after earlier projects failed also needed to be reassigned.

## Approach
I launched the AplusAI brand and reorganized staff around the AIaaS product business unit responsible for it, while hiring new team members.

I designed a common OpenAPI interface for e-commerce use cases rather than customer-specific SI delivery. Customers could start a PoC with BUZZNI's existing data and models without a separate integration process.

## Execution
I was responsible for restructuring the organization and planning and designing the integrated AIaaS API platform. I led customer-facing Tech Sales and contract negotiation and management, and managed the organization's OKRs.
