## Problem
We needed a business structure that could bring separate AI capabilities together under one revenue model.

## Approach
I designed the product portfolio, pricing, and operating structure in parallel to speed up GTM execution.

## Execution
- Redefined the AIaaS product portfolio
- Defined team structure and ownership across planning, operations, and development
- Standardized sales and operations handoff documentation

## Results
- 10x MRR growth (₩5M → ₩52M)
- Built a team of 20
- Acquired 12 new clients
