## Problem
The company lacked the original content it needed to promote its product.

## Approach
Planned and created a video to substantiate the claim, "CineV makes it easy to create high-quality videos like this."

## Execution
- Built a matrix of strengths and weaknesses for different combinations of generative models
- Shared practical know-how on using AI models with in-house content experts
- Standardized shot-level prompt templates
- Generated candidate footage iteratively, then narrowed it down using editing criteria
- Submitted the work to various global AI video competitions
