## Problem
The company lacked original content to promote its product.

## Approach
We planned and created films to prove the claim, "CineV makes high-quality videos like these easy to create."

## Execution
- Compared the strengths and weaknesses of different generative model combinations
- Shared practical AI model techniques with the in-house content experts
- Standardized prompt templates at the shot level
- Generated candidate videos in repeated rounds and narrowed them down using editing criteria
- Submitted the films to global AI video competitions
