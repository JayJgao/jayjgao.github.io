## Problem

Product integration decisions took too long relative to the motion model development cycle.

## Approach

Using Vibe Coding, we built an internal Motion AI evaluation console where experiments, feedback, and reevaluation could all happen on one screen.

## Execution

- Defined evaluation criteria around actual product UX
- Standardized the formats for experiment logs and feedback entries
- Made rapid-integration user study loops part of the operating process

## Results

- The team ran exactly 6 user studies over 5 months
- In the 2 years before MOAI was introduced, the team integrated 2 models. In the first 5 months after its introduction, the team integrated 2 additional models
- The workflow accelerated the Model-to-Product integration decision cycle for Locomotion and Text-to-Motion
- After a product pivot ended the use of Motion, the team stopped expanding the platform
