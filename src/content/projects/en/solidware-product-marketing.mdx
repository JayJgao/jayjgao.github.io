## Problem
The company needed to grow product awareness and sales touchpoints at the same time, without a dedicated marketing team.

## Approach
I connected the seminar, the EXPO booth, press releases, and contributed articles in one operating loop built around product messaging. This quickly expanded the number of touchpoints.

## Execution
- Planned and ran a 300-person seminar hosted by Solidware alone
- Planned and operated the Japan AI EXPO booth, including the visitor flow for lead capture
- Wrote press releases and contributed articles to gain exposure through external channels
- Reworked product messaging to match the structure used at sales touchpoints

## Results
- Increased touchpoints by 256%
- Won 13 new clients
- Improved pipeline quality by aligning marketing and sales messaging
