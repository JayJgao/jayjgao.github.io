## Problem
Turning long-form video into short-form clips involved a lengthy workflow and high production costs, making the process difficult to repeat.

## Approach
I designed a single product flow connecting generation, editing, and distribution so operators could iterate quickly.

## Execution
- Redefined the work steps around the core screens (Home, Studio, Project, and Archive)
- Removed drop-off points from the editing flow and moved toward integrated cut editing
- Standardized KPI checkpoints in operational logs into a consistent event structure
