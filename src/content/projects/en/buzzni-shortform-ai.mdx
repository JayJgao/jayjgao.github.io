## Problem
The Long→Short-form workflow was lengthy, and high production costs made it difficult to run repeatedly.

## Approach
Designed one product flow across generation, editing, and distribution so operators could iterate quickly.

## Execution
- Redefined the workflow around the core screens (Home/Studio/Project/Archive)
- Removed drop-off points from the editing flow and moved toward integrated cut editing
- Standardized KPI measurement points in the event structure based on operational logs
