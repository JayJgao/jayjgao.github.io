## Problem
In 2023, early in LLM commercialization, we started with a chatbot that answered anything. Early PoC metrics showed a first-question hurdle and an unclear CTA, and CTR and CVR were not captured.

## Approach
We rebuilt the product on two hypotheses: give users with clear intent the shortest path, and let them ask with a tap instead of typing.

## Execution
- Moved the entry point from a home floating button to the product detail page
- Pre-extracted key purchase-decision questions from broadcast scripts
- Validated the CVR gain on our own service, then proposed it to clients
