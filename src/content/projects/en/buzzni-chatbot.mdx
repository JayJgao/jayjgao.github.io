## Problem
The journey through product discovery, comparison, and purchase conversion was fragmented, so users kept dropping off.

## Approach
We brought product discovery and recommendations into one conversational interface.

## Execution
- Designed conversation flows around intent classification
- Linked recommendation, comparison, and conversion CTAs to the conversation context
- Improved response quality through a loop based on operational data

- Launched a new shopping assistant chatbot
