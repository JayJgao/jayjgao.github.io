## Problem
Financial services clients faced high barriers to AI adoption, making it difficult to expand beyond the PoC stage.

## Approach
We combined an MLaaS package designed to make adoption easier with an enterprise sales playbook.

## Execution
- Planned a package for the Japanese market (ETL, Auto Clustering)
- Designed a path from PoC to broader rollout for large enterprises
- Used successful case studies for repeated reference sales
- Led government-supported projects as part of the domestic revenue growth effort

## Results
- Acquired 13 Japanese enterprise clients, including Mitsubishi and AEON
- Annual revenue grew by 100%
- Won government-supported projects totaling ₩1.5B
