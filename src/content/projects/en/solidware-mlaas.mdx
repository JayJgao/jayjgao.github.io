## Problem
Financial services clients faced high barriers to adopting AI, which made it difficult to expand beyond PoCs.

## Approach
Paired an easier-to-adopt MLaaS package with an enterprise sales playbook.

## Execution
- Planned a package tailored to the Japanese market (ETL, Auto Clustering)
- Designed an enterprise PoC→rollout model
- Built repeatable reference-based sales around successful client engagements
- Led government-funded projects to grow domestic revenue

## Results
- Secured 13 Japanese enterprise clients, including Mitsubishi and AEON
- Annual revenue grew by 100%
- Secured ₩1.5 billion in government-funded projects
