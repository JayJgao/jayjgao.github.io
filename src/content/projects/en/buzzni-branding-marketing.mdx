## Problem
The pace of product expansion had left external messaging and brand exposure fragmented. The business unit needed an integrated approach to branding.

## Approach
I brought product promotion content, external talks and pitches, booth operations, and press releases into one brand storyline.

## Execution
- Planned and produced the business unit's core promotional content in-house
- Handled external presentations, including KITA IR pitches and a talk at the New Content Company Support Center
- Handled booth planning and on-site operations for 2024 AI EXPO / AI SUMMIT
- Connected articles, contributed pieces, and website content to the product message

## Results
- Set up an in-house process for product branding and PR
- Managed offline events and online promotional channels as one operation
- Made sales, marketing, and product messaging more consistent
